Our madrasa has these main features, and also many other features

Memorization of the Quran

Becoming a true scholar requires memorizing the original texts of the Qur’an and Sunnah. Therefore, Hifz al-Qur’an is a core part of the Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy syllabus.Students will memorize according to their ability: some may complete the whole Qur’an, others 20, 10, or at least 5 juz, which is compulsory for everyone.

Skilled in Arabic, English, and Bangla.

Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy nurtures language mastery as a tool for da‘wah and meeting contemporary challenges. Through Arabic, English, and Bangla instruction — enriched by a Language Club — students will gain fluency to access Qur’an and Hadith in their original texts and convey that wisdom in three tongues, in shā’ Allāh.

Islamic morals and values

One of Islam’s core teachings is to live by knowledge, nurture good manners, and uphold values. To help students grow with practice, character, and taqwa, Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy has shaped its teaching and environment accordingly.

Parental Advice Program

A student’s progress in learning and character requires joint efforts from teachers and parents. Hence, Al-Ishara Islami Academy holds regular Teacher–Parent Meetings, where Shaykh Ahmadullah (Hafizahullah) offers guidance and engages with parents.

Facilities of our Madrasa

The madrasa provides its own transport facilities

For the convenience of its students, Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy possesses four dedicated buses. These buses operate along three routes—Aftabnagar Gate, Uttar Badda, and Notun Bazar—picking up and dropping off students from various points. From the coming academic year, the number of buses will be increased as needed, In shā Allāh.

Student Residential Facility

To accommodate students arriving from various regions of the country, Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy provides limited residential facilities. The institution remains committed to fostering academic excellence, nurturing moral values, serving nutritious meals, and maintaining a pleasant and conducive environment for learning. This residential provision is reserved solely for male students of grade two and higher.

Scholarship Opportunities

Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy extends full scholarship opportunities to poor and orphaned yet meritorious students. Where a student is both eligible for Zakat and gifted in learning, the Al-Ishara Foundation, Alhamdulillah, graciously bears all expenses through its Zakat Fund, upon the earnest request of the guardian. This noble provision is reserved for up to 20% of the student body.

Concession for siblings

At Al-Ishara Islami Acadimy, when two children from the same parents are enrolled, the second child enjoys a 50% concession on both admission and tuition fees. This initiative reflects our commitment to easing the financial burden of parents. It is our way of encouraging families to provide quality Islamic and academic education for all their children.
